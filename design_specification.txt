### 🌐 WEBSITE DESIGN SPECIFICATION: GLOBAL REACH EXPORTS (GRE) | UPDATED GLASSMORPHISM SYSTEM

**CORE OBJECTIVE:**  
Create a high-end, "editorial" trade and logistics platform that balances industrial reliability with modern digital aesthetics. The design must evoke trust, global scale, and precision.

---

#### **1. COLOR PALETTE & THEME:**
- **Primary Navy:** `#19315A`
- **Accent Blue:** `#559EB8`
- **Mid Blue:** `#97C9DC`
- **Light Blue:** `#D6EAF3`
- **Base Background:** `#F4F8FA`

---

#### **2. TYPOGRAPHY:**
- **Headings:** `Unbounded`, sans-serif (Extra Bold 900 / Medium 500-600)
- **Body Text:** `Montserrat`, sans-serif (Regular 400 / Semi-Bold 600)

---

#### **3. GLASSMORPHISM SYSTEM (UPDATED & CONTEXT-MAPPED)**

**Base Glass Formula (Desktop):**
```css
background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35);
backdrop-filter: blur(22px);
-webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(22px);
border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5);
border-radius: 16px;
box-shadow: 
  0 8px 32px rgba(25, 49, 90, 0.08),
  inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6);
```

**Glass Variants by Context:**
| Component | Background | Blur | Border | Shadow | Use Case |
|-----------|------------|------|--------|--------|----------|
| **Hero Overlays** | `rgba(255,255,255,0.30)` | `24px` | `rgba(255,255,255,0.55)` | `0 12px 40px rgba(25,49,90,0.12)` | Floating text panels over imagery |
| **Floating Cards / Badges** | `rgba(255,255,255,0.40)` | `18px` | `rgba(255,255,255,0.45)` | `0 6px 24px rgba(25,49,90,0.06)` | Tooltips, stats, badges |
| **Navbar Pill** | `rgba(244,248,250,0.75)` | `16px` | `rgba(255,255,255,0.6)` | `0 4px 16px rgba(25,49,90,0.05)` | Sticky nav links |
| **Mobile Glass** | `rgba(255,255,255,0.55)` | `10px` | `rgba(255,255,255,0.4)` | `0 4px 12px rgba(25,49,90,0.08)` | Mobile fallbacks |

**Glass Interaction States:**
- **Hover:** `transform: translateY(-4px); box-shadow: 0 16px 48px rgba(25,49,90,0.15); border-color: rgba(255,255,255,0.7);`
- **Active/Pressed:** `transform: translateY(-1px); box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(25,49,90,0.1);`
- **Transition:** `all 0.35s cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.46, 0.45, 0.94);`

**Typography on Glass:**
- Primary text: `#19315A` (Navy) at 90-100% opacity
- Secondary/labels: `#559EB8` (Accent Blue) at 85% opacity
- Add `text-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(255,255,255,0.5)` when over dark regions

---

#### **4. SIGNATURE LAYOUTS (GLASS-INTEGRATED)**

**🔹 Editorial Split-Hero:**
- Left column: Clean typography on `#F4F8FA` (no glass)
- Right column: Full-bleed imagery with blue monochrome filter
- **Glass Application:** 
  - Overlays (Mission, Commodities) use "Hero Overlay" formula
  - Aligned to image grid, never overlapping key focal points

**🔹 Honeycomb Interactive Grid:**
- Base cards: Solid `#F4F8FA` with `1px solid #D6EAF3` borders (no glass)
- **Glass Application:** Only on hover: `rgba(25,49,90,0.6)` with `blur(8px)` + white text

**🔹 Editorial 3-Column Grid:**
- Keep sharp `1px solid #e2e8f0` dividers (no glass)

---

#### **5. COMPONENT SPECS (UPDATED)**

**🔹 Navbar:**
- Sticky header: `background: rgba(244,248,250,0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(16px);`
- Nav links contained in pill: `background: rgba(255,255,255,0.5); border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.4);`

**🔹 3D Interactive Buttons:**
- Solid gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #1c3866 0%, #14284d 100%)`
- Place on glass containers with `padding: 8px` to prevent visual clash

**🔹 Contact Form:**
- Inputs: `background: rgba(255,255,255,0.6); backdrop-filter: blur(8px); border: 1px solid #D6EAF3;`

**🔹 Footer:**
- Minimalist white: `background: #ffffff;` (no glass)

---

#### **6. PERFORMANCE & RESPONSIVENESS (GLASS-OPTIMIZED)**

- **Max-width:** `1200px`
- **Mobile (<768px):** 
  - Reduce blur to 10px. Replace heavy glass with solid fallbacks where necessary.
- **Fallbacks:** 
  - Use `@supports not (backdrop-filter: blur(1px))` for solid backgrounds.
- **Accessibility:** 
  - Maintain WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1) for all text on glass.
